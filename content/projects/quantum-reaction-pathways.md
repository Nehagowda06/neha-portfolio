Quantum Reaction Pathways is a physics-driven computational tool for exploring catalytic reaction pathways using first-principles quantum dynamics.

The system allows users to construct candidate Hamiltonians and simulate reaction pathways through tunable physical parameters.

Key capabilities include:

• Hamiltonian generation for candidate catalytic systems  
• Interactive parameter tuning for quantum effects  
• Reaction pathway simulation and screening  
• Ranking of candidate configurations based on stability metrics
